## Plan at a glance

Team: **2 backend (BE-A, BE-B) + 2 Flutter (FE-A, FE-B)**, fully dedicated.

- **Phase 1 — Jul 15:** `tc-api-gateway`, `tc-cardload-service`, `tc-card-network-service`,
  `tc-ledger-service`, `tc-settlement-reconciliation-service` **functionally complete (no
  hardening)** — the end-to-end top-up works in sandbox. Flutter builds the full portal UI
  against mocks in parallel.
- **Phase 2 — Jul 30:** `tc-admin-service` backend + **portal integration** (the portal
  goes live against the real backend).
- **Phase 3 — after Jul 30:** hardening (security/PCI, perf, gateway custom plugins, full
  reversal/adjustment/refund, production cert).

**Feasibility:** the 5 Phase-1 services are ~10 developer-weeks of *functional* work against
~11 dev-weeks of backend capacity — achievable **only if "no hardening" is held strictly**
and the external sandboxes are ready on day 1. There is little slack; **reconciliation is
the long pole most likely to slip** (see [risks](#assumptions--risks)).

Track split: **BE-A** = Visa rails + reconciliation; **BE-B** = gateway + orchestrator +
ledger. Flutter works **contract-first** against mocks. Days 1–2 both backend devs pair on
foundations, then split.

## Assumptions & risks

1. **"No hardening" is load-bearing.** Phase 1 fits only as *functional happy path* — minimal
   error handling, config-driven limits, no custom gateway plugins, no security/perf pass.
   Treat outputs as sandbox-grade until Phase 3.
2. **Reconciliation is the long pole.** It sits in BE-A's back half (Jul 2–15) and can only be
   fully tested once card-network/cardload emit events (~Jul 8+). If anything upstream slips,
   **recon is the first item to slide past Jul 15** — descope its matching depth before
   sacrificing the rails.
3. **External sandboxes on day 1 are the critical path.** Visa Developer (VDP) + test BIN and
   Cybersource sandbox credentials must be live from Jun 9; the AFT/3DS tracks and the Jul 15
   demo depend on them.
4. **Limits via config until Phase 2.** Authorization limits are seeded/config-managed until
   the admin CRUD UI ships Jul 16–30.
5. **Portal integrates in Phase 2.** Through Jul 15 the portal is UI-complete on mocks; it
   talks to a real `tc-admin-service` only from Jul 16.
6. **Little slack.** With 2 backend devs this is near-full utilization — a sustained blocker
   (env, sandbox, or a hard Visa/Cybersource integration bug) is the realistic threat to the
   Jul 15 date, not coding speed.
