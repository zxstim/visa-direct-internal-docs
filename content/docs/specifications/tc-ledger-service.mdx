## Purpose & scope

`tc-ledger-service` is the **financial system of record**. It is an immutable,
double-entry ledger that records every money movement in the top-up lifecycle so the
business always knows, to the cent: what Visa owes us, what we have advanced to customers,
what fees we earned, and what is still unsettled.

In the sequence diagram its explicit input is the **prefunding outcome** — PVCB Core
Banking forwards "details of the completed prefunding transaction" and "details of the
failed prefunding transaction" to this service. But a ledger that only saw prefunding
would not balance, so this service books the **whole money lifecycle**:

1. The **AFT receivable** (funds pulled from the card → Visa owes us at settlement).
2. The **prefunding advance** (we credit the tourist's VND account immediately, from our
   own liquidity, because Visa settles T+n).
3. **Settlement clearing** (Visa pays us T+n → the receivable clears, the pool is repaid).
4. **Reversals / adjustments / refunds** (corrections must net correctly).

It also enforces the **prefunding liquidity exposure limit** — the control behind the
program's "advanced funding" prerequisite (the acquirer must hold enough liquidity for
2–3 days of volume).

> The ledger is **authoritative but not the matcher**. Matching the Visa settlement file
> against ledger entries is done by `tc-settlement-reconciliation-service`, which instructs
> this ledger to post clearing entries. See [Settlement interface](#settlement--reconciliation-interface).

## Technical stack

JDK 25, Spring Boot 4.0+ (Maven), Spring Data JPA on **MySQL 8.4 LTS (InnoDB)** with `DECIMAL`
money columns and strict SQL mode; messaging via **Spring Cloud Stream** (broker-abstracted). The ledger is an **event consumer** — it books entries in response to events from
card-network, the core-banking adapter, and reconciliation (see
[Architecture & service interaction](/docs/specifications/architecture)). Posting is a single
local DB transaction; consumers are **idempotent**.

## Position in the flow

```
tc-card-network-service ──AFT approved / reversed / adjusted / refunded──▶ ┐
PVCB Core Banking ──────prefunding completed / failed───────────────────▶ ├─ tc-ledger-service
tc-settlement-reconciliation-service ──matched settlement lines──────────▶ ┘   (post balanced entries)
                                                                               │
                                                                  exposure / balances / trial balance
                                                                               ▼
                                                                 treasury & tc-admin-service views
```

## Core principles

- **Double-entry & always balanced.** Every business event posts a journal entry whose
  legs sum to **zero per currency**. A non-balancing entry is rejected, never stored.
- **Immutable / append-only.** Entries are never edited or deleted. Corrections are made
  with **reversing entries** that reference the original. Full audit trail.
- **Idempotent / exactly-once.** Each business event posts once; replays are no-ops. Money
  events must never be double-booked.
- **Multi-currency.** Postings are per-currency (USD pulled, VND funded); FX is an explicit
  linked pair of legs at a recorded rate.
- **Derivable balances.** Account balances are reconstructable from the journal at any time;
  materialized balances are an optimization, not the source of truth.
- **Fail closed.** If an event cannot be posted atomically and balanced, it is rejected and
  surfaced — never partially applied.

## Account model (chart of accounts)

Illustrative — finalize the exact chart with finance/treasury. Each account has a type and
a normal side; assets/expenses are debit-normal, liabilities/revenue/equity credit-normal.

| Account | Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `visa_receivable` | Asset | Owed by Visa from approved AFTs, until settled (USD) |
| `nostro_settlement` | Asset | Bank/nostro where the Visa settlement lands |
| `prefunding_pool` | Asset | Our liquidity used to advance funds to customers |
| `customer_funds_payable` | Liability | Obligation to make funds available to the customer |
| `fx_clearing` | Clearing | Bridges the USD and VND legs of a conversion |
| `fee_income` | Revenue | Service / FX-margin fees we earn |
| `scheme_fees_expense` | Expense | Visa scheme fees |
| `fx_gain_loss` | Revenue/Expense | Realized FX difference at settlement |
| `suspense` | Clearing | In-flight / unmatched / exception holding |

## Money lifecycle & postings

Canonical legs per event (FX and fee legs simplified; finalize with finance). Dr = debit,
Cr = credit.

| Event | Postings |
| --- | --- |
| **AFT approved** (USD pulled) | Dr `visa_receivable` (USD) · Cr `customer_funds_payable` (USD) |
| **FX conversion** (USD→VND) | Dr `customer_funds_payable` (USD) · Cr `fx_clearing` (USD); Dr `fx_clearing` (VND) · Cr `customer_funds_payable` (VND) · Cr `fee_income` (FX margin) |
| **Prefunding completed** (VND advanced) | Dr `customer_funds_payable` (VND) · Cr `prefunding_pool` (VND) |
| **Prefunding failed** | No funding leg; flag the transaction and hold in `suspense`; trigger AFT reversal (see below) |
| **Settlement received** (T+n) | Dr `nostro_settlement` (USD) · Cr `visa_receivable` (USD); realize `fx_gain_loss`; replenish `prefunding_pool` |
| **AFT reversal** | Reversing entries of the AFT/funding legs, referencing the original entry |
| **Adjustment / refund (OCT)** | Booked as new balanced entries referencing the original `transactionId` |

> **Worked example (illustrative).** A USD 100 top-up at 25,000 VND/USD with a 1% FX
> margin: on AFT approval, `visa_receivable` +100 USD / `customer_funds_payable` +100 USD.
> After FX, the customer is owed 2,475,000 VND and `fee_income` earns 25,000 VND. On
> prefunding completion, `customer_funds_payable` −2,475,000 VND / `prefunding_pool`
> −2,475,000 VND. At settlement, `nostro_settlement` +100 USD / `visa_receivable` −100 USD
> and the pool is replenished. Exact legs/rounding/fee treatment are owned by finance.

## Prefunding ingestion (the diagram's explicit input)

PVCB Core Banking forwards every prefunding outcome here:

- **Completed** → post the prefunding advance legs; the transaction's funding is now
  recognized. Mark the AFT receivable as backing an outstanding advance (counts toward
  [exposure](#prefunding-liquidity-exposure-limit)) until settlement clears it.
- **Failed** → **do not** book a funding leg. The AFT was already approved (funds pulled),
  so an un-fundable transaction is a break: hold it in `suspense`, raise an exception, and
  signal `tc-card-network-service` to **reverse the AFT** so no funds are left pulled from
  the cardholder. The ledger records the reversal when it is confirmed.

Both events are **idempotent** on the business `transactionId` + event type.

## Prefunding (liquidity) exposure limit

The control behind advanced funding: track the **outstanding prefunded-but-unsettled
exposure** and cap it.

- `exposure = Σ prefunding advances not yet cleared by settlement`.
- A **configurable ceiling** (sized to 2–3 days of expected volume, per corridor/currency).
- When exposure approaches the ceiling: warn treasury; at the ceiling: **reject / throttle**
  new prefunding and signal upstream (`tc-cardload-service` / `tc-card-network-service`) so
  new top-ups are held rather than funded beyond available liquidity.
- Expose real-time exposure and balances to treasury and `tc-admin-service`.
- **Fail closed:** if exposure cannot be computed, hold new prefunding.

```yaml
ledger:
  exposure:
    ceiling:        { USD: 5000000 }     # outstanding prefunded cap
    warnThreshold:  0.8                   # warn treasury at 80%
    perCurrency:    true
    failClosed:     true
  posting:
    requireBalanced: true                 # reject any entry that doesn't net to zero
    enforceIdempotency: true
```

## Idempotency & exactly-once posting

- Every inbound event carries a unique key (`transactionId` + `eventType`, or an explicit
  `Idempotency-Key`). The ledger dedupes on it; a replay returns the existing journal entry.
- Posting is a single atomic database transaction: write the journal entry + all legs +
  update materialized balances, or nothing.
- A balanced-entry constraint (`Σ debits == Σ credits` per currency) is enforced at write
  time.

## Immutability, corrections & audit

- Journal entries and legs are **append-only** — no `UPDATE`/`DELETE`.
- A correction is a **reversing entry** that references `reversesEntryId`; the original
  stays visible. This preserves a defensible audit trail for finance and regulators.
- Every entry records: source service, business `transactionId`, event type, actor, and
  timestamp.

## Settlement & reconciliation interface

- `tc-settlement-reconciliation-service` matches the Visa settlement file lines against
  `visa_receivable` entries and publishes a **`settlement.matched`** event, which this
  ledger consumes to post the **settlement-received** clearing entries (and FX gain/loss).
- **Breaks** (settlement line with no matching ledger entry, or vice versa) are posted to
  `suspense` and raised as exceptions for ops — never silently dropped.
- The ledger exposes per-transaction state so reconciliation can match on Visa
  `transactionIdentifier` / RRN / amount.

## Data model

- `accounts` — id, type, normal side, currency policy.
- `journal_entries` — id, business `transactionId`, eventType, `reversesEntryId?`,
  idempotencyKey (unique), createdAt, source. **Append-only.**
- `postings` — entryId, accountId, direction (Dr/Cr), amount, currency. Sum per (entry,
  currency) must be zero.
- `account_balances` — materialized per (account, currency); rebuildable from `postings`.
- `exposure` — derived/materialized outstanding-prefunded per currency.

## Database schema

**MySQL 8.4 LTS / InnoDB, `utf8mb4`.** Design decisions:

- Classic double-entry: `journal_entry` (1) → `posting` (N). Every entry's postings sum to
  **zero per currency** — enforced at write; a periodic trial-balance job re-verifies.
- **Append-only:** `journal_entry`/`posting` are never updated or deleted; corrections are
  **reversing entries** linked via `reverses_entry_id`.
- **Idempotent:** unique `idempotency_key` on `journal_entry` (a replayed event posts once),
  plus the messaging inbox (`processed_event`).
- **Multi-currency:** postings are per-currency; `account_balance` is materialized per
  `(account, currency)` and rebuildable from `posting`. Money as `DECIMAL(23,4)`.
- `exposure` materializes outstanding prefunded-but-unsettled per currency vs the ceiling
  (from `limits.updated`).

```
account ◀── posting ──▶ journal_entry ──(self) reverses_entry_id
   │                         ▲
account_balance        (1 entry → N postings, balanced per currency)
   exposure · outbox · processed_event   (standalone)
```

```sql
CREATE TABLE account (
  id          BIGINT UNSIGNED AUTO_INCREMENT PRIMARY KEY,
  code        VARCHAR(64)  NOT NULL,    -- 'visa_receivable', 'prefunding_pool', ...
  name        VARCHAR(128) NOT NULL,
  type        VARCHAR(20)  NOT NULL,    -- ASSET/LIABILITY/REVENUE/EXPENSE/EQUITY/CLEARING
  normal_side VARCHAR(6)   NOT NULL,    -- DEBIT/CREDIT
  active      BOOLEAN      NOT NULL DEFAULT TRUE,
  created_at  DATETIME(3)  NOT NULL DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP(3),
  UNIQUE KEY uk_account_code (code),
  CONSTRAINT chk_account_type CHECK (type IN ('ASSET','LIABILITY','REVENUE','EXPENSE','EQUITY','CLEARING')),
  CONSTRAINT chk_account_side CHECK (normal_side IN ('DEBIT','CREDIT'))
) ENGINE=InnoDB DEFAULT CHARSET=utf8mb4;

CREATE TABLE journal_entry (                       -- append-only
  id                BIGINT UNSIGNED AUTO_INCREMENT PRIMARY KEY,
  entry_ref         CHAR(36)     NOT NULL,         -- unique business id
  idempotency_key   VARCHAR(96)  NOT NULL,         -- e.g. <transaction_ref>:<event_type>
  event_type        VARCHAR(40)  NOT NULL,         -- AFT_APPROVED/FX_CONVERSION/PREFUNDING_COMPLETED/SETTLEMENT_RECEIVED/REVERSAL/ADJUSTMENT/REFUND
  transaction_ref   VARCHAR(48)  NULL,             -- business topup id (trace spine)
  correlation_id    VARCHAR(64)  NULL,             -- trace id (= OpenTelemetry trace), from the event envelope
  reverses_entry_id BIGINT UNSIGNED NULL,          -- correction → original entry
  source            VARCHAR(40)  NOT NULL,         -- producing service
  description       VARCHAR(255) NULL,
  created_at        DATETIME(3)  NOT NULL DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP(3),
  UNIQUE KEY uk_je_ref  (entry_ref),
  UNIQUE KEY uk_je_idem (idempotency_key),
  KEY idx_je_txn (transaction_ref),
  KEY idx_je_corr (correlation_id),
  KEY idx_je_created (created_at),
  CONSTRAINT fk_je_reverses FOREIGN KEY (reverses_entry_id) REFERENCES journal_entry(id)
) ENGINE=InnoDB DEFAULT CHARSET=utf8mb4;

CREATE TABLE posting (
  id               BIGINT UNSIGNED AUTO_INCREMENT PRIMARY KEY,
  journal_entry_id BIGINT UNSIGNED NOT NULL,
  account_id       BIGINT UNSIGNED NOT NULL,
  direction        VARCHAR(6)    NOT NULL,         -- DEBIT/CREDIT
  amount           DECIMAL(23,4) NOT NULL,         -- positive; side given by direction
  currency         CHAR(3)       NOT NULL,
  reference        VARCHAR(64)   NULL,             -- sub-ledger key (customer/card/txn)
  created_at       DATETIME(3)   NOT NULL DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP(3),
  KEY idx_posting_entry (journal_entry_id),
  KEY idx_posting_account_ccy (account_id, currency),
  KEY idx_posting_reference (reference),
  CONSTRAINT fk_posting_entry   FOREIGN KEY (journal_entry_id) REFERENCES journal_entry(id),
  CONSTRAINT fk_posting_account FOREIGN KEY (account_id) REFERENCES account(id),
  CONSTRAINT chk_posting_dir CHECK (direction IN ('DEBIT','CREDIT')),
  CONSTRAINT chk_posting_amt CHECK (amount > 0)
) ENGINE=InnoDB DEFAULT CHARSET=utf8mb4;

CREATE TABLE account_balance (                     -- materialized; rebuildable from posting
  account_id BIGINT UNSIGNED NOT NULL,
  currency   CHAR(3)        NOT NULL,
  balance    DECIMAL(23,4)  NOT NULL DEFAULT 0,    -- signed in the account's normal-side convention
  updated_at DATETIME(3)    NOT NULL DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP(3) ON UPDATE CURRENT_TIMESTAMP(3),
  version    INT            NOT NULL DEFAULT 0,
  PRIMARY KEY (account_id, currency),
  CONSTRAINT fk_bal_account FOREIGN KEY (account_id) REFERENCES account(id)
) ENGINE=InnoDB DEFAULT CHARSET=utf8mb4;

CREATE TABLE exposure (
  currency           CHAR(3)       NOT NULL PRIMARY KEY,
  outstanding_amount DECIMAL(23,4) NOT NULL DEFAULT 0,   -- prefunded-but-unsettled
  ceiling_amount     DECIMAL(23,4) NULL,                 -- from limits.updated
  updated_at         DATETIME(3)   NOT NULL DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP(3) ON UPDATE CURRENT_TIMESTAMP(3)
) ENGINE=InnoDB DEFAULT CHARSET=utf8mb4;

CREATE TABLE outbox (
  id           BIGINT UNSIGNED AUTO_INCREMENT PRIMARY KEY,
  event_id     CHAR(36)     NOT NULL,
  event_type   VARCHAR(64)  NOT NULL,    -- prefunding.exposure.updated
  aggregate_id VARCHAR(48)  NOT NULL,
  payload      JSON         NOT NULL,
  status       VARCHAR(20)  NOT NULL DEFAULT 'PENDING',
  attempts     INT          NOT NULL DEFAULT 0,
  available_at DATETIME(3)  NOT NULL DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP(3),
  published_at DATETIME(3)  NULL,
  created_at   DATETIME(3)  NOT NULL DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP(3),
  UNIQUE KEY uk_outbox_event (event_id),
  KEY idx_outbox_dispatch (status, available_at),
  CONSTRAINT chk_outbox_status CHECK (status IN ('PENDING','PUBLISHED','FAILED'))
) ENGINE=InnoDB DEFAULT CHARSET=utf8mb4;

CREATE TABLE processed_event (
  event_id     CHAR(36)    NOT NULL PRIMARY KEY,   -- aft.*/prefunding.*/settlement.matched/limits.updated
  event_type   VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL,
  reference    VARCHAR(64) NULL,
  processed_at DATETIME(3) NOT NULL DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP(3)
) ENGINE=InnoDB DEFAULT CHARSET=utf8mb4;
```

## Consistency, integrity & period close

- Balanced-entry invariant enforced per write; periodic **trial-balance** job verifies the
  whole book nets to zero per currency.
- Materialized balances reconciled against the journal on a schedule; any drift alerts.
- Period close locks a reporting window; post-close corrections use reversing entries dated
  in the open period.

## Error handling & fail-closed

- Reject (never partially post) any entry that is unbalanced, duplicates an idempotency key
  with a different payload, or references a missing account.
- Fail closed on exposure-store or DB unavailability for **new prefunding/postings**;
  read-only queries may serve last-known balances clearly marked as such.
- All rejects emit metrics + structured logs for ops.

## Observability

- Metrics: posting throughput, rejected/unbalanced entries, outstanding exposure vs
  ceiling, suspense balance, settlement-match rate, FX gain/loss.
- Alert on: exposure breaching `warnThreshold`/ceiling, non-zero/growing `suspense`,
  trial-balance drift, prefunding-failure spikes, and any fail-closed event.
- Structured, append-only audit log to SIEM; correlate on business `transactionId`.

## Events

Postings are driven by events (see [Architecture](/docs/specifications/architecture)). The
`POST /v1/events/*` entries in [Service API](#service-api) are the logical message handlers
(functional `Consumer` beans) plus a replay/admin path; in production they are delivered by
the broker, not called directly. Consumers are **idempotent** on `eventId` (+ `transactionId`
+ event type); ledger postings are ordered by `accountId` (partition key).

| Direction | Event | Counterpart |
| --- | --- | --- |
| consumes | `aft.completed` / `aft.reversed` / `aft.adjusted` / `aft.refunded` | card-network |
| consumes | `prefunding.completed` / `prefunding.failed` | core-banking adapter |
| consumes | `settlement.matched` | reconciliation |
| consumes | `limits.updated` (prefunding exposure ceiling) | admin |
| produces | `prefunding.exposure.updated` (optional, for dashboards) | admin |
